HOW IT FEELS

Connect → Bloom → Discover

One gesture starts the whole experience. The connection is immediate; what appears after it is the surprise.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Trace a path through the stars and build a chain with a single continuous gesture.

  2. 02

    Bloom

    Your play opens into rings, particles, flowers, fireworks, and other game-native light effects.

  3. 03

    Discover

    Some moments reveal SPECIALs that are recorded in your album and give you another reason to keep looking.

SPECIAL

The sky has more to find.

HOSHITSUNAGI is built around discovery rather than a fixed visual reward. Different plays can open into different moments, while discovered SPECIALs are collected in your album.

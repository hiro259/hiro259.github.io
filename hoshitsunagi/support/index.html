What to include

When reporting a problem, the following information can help identify what happened:

  • Your HOSHITSUNAGI app version
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • What you were doing immediately before the issue occurred
  • A screenshot or screen recording, when useful

Please do not send personal information or backup contents that are not necessary for support.
